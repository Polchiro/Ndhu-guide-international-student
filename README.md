# Getting Started at NDHU: A Guide for International Students

## 1. Arriving at NDHU

### Getting from Taoyuan Airport to NDHU

1. **Taoyuan Airport to Taipei Main Station:** Take the Airport MRT (NT$160).
2. **Taipei Main Station to Hualien:** Take a Taiwan Railways (TRA) train. Check the [official TRA timetable and booking website](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip?lang=EN_US).
3. **Choose one of two routes to NDHU:**
   - **Route A:** Get off at Hualien Station and take Bus 301 to NDHU. Use the Taiwan Bus+ app to check bus schedules. A taxi costs approximately NT$300–500.
   - **Route B:** Get off at Zhixue Station. NDHU is about 1.5 km away on foot, so walking is not recommended if you have luggage. A taxi costs approximately NT$150, although some drivers may not accept such a short trip.

### Booking Your Train Ticket

**Option 1: Taiwan Railway e-booking app**

1. Download the official Taiwan Railway e-booking app before arriving in Taiwan.
2. Search for trains from Taipei to Hualien Station or Zhixue Station, then select your travel date and time.
3. Enter your passport details and pay for your ticket.
4. Keep your electronic ticket or QR code ready if the app provides one. Follow the instructions in the app for entering the station.

*Look for the official Taiwan Railway e-booking app in the App Store.*

**Option 2: Book through the TRA website**

Visit the [official TRA website](https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip?lang=EN_US) and book using your passport details. If you receive a booking code rather than an electronic ticket, collect your physical ticket at a station ticket counter or an eligible convenience store according to the booking instructions. Bring your passport and booking code.

### What I Wish I'd Known

If you live in Dorm VI or Dorm VII, prepare your own bedding. You will need a fitted bedsheet, a mattress suitable for a 194 × 82 cm bed, a pillow with a pillowcase, and a blanket. You can purchase these items at a supermarket outside the campus.

### Important Tips

**Your ARC (Alien Resident Certificate)**

- Apply as soon as possible through the [National Immigration Agency's online application system for foreign students](https://coa.immigration.gov.tw/coa-frontend/student/entry?lang=en).
- Make sure you have mobile data so you can navigate to the collection location. If you do not have mobile data yet, prepare the directions in advance or go with a friend.
- Collect your ARC at the NIA Hualien County Service Center.

  **Address:** 5F, No. 371, Zhongshan Road, Hualien City, Hualien County 970009, Taiwan  
  **Opening hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, including lunchtime.

**Bus Passes**

If you use buses frequently, consider buying an EasyCard and purchasing a monthly pass at 7-Eleven. A monthly pass can be cheaper than paying for individual rides. Each pass is valid for 30 days and provides unlimited rides on eligible services within its coverage area.

| Pass | Price | Coverage |
|---|---:|---|
| Hualien Basic Monthly Pass | NT$199 | Eligible local Hualien buses and eligible TRA services between Heping and Fuli stations |
| Hualien Extended Monthly Pass | NT$399 | Basic Pass coverage, plus eligible intercity and highway bus routes within Hualien County and selected Hualien–Taitung services |

Check the current coverage and purchasing requirements before buying a pass.

## 2. Phone Plans

I recommend considering the NT$600-per-month contract with Chunghwa Telecom (中華電信) if it is available to you and the contract terms suit your needs. In my opinion, Chunghwa Telecom is one of Taiwan's best telecom providers.

Registration Day is a convenient opportunity to get mobile data, potentially without an ARC. If you can sign up during this period, take advantage of it.

If you miss this opportunity, do not worry. After receiving your ARC and student ID card, visit a Chunghwa Telecom service center and ask about available student plans. Bring your passport, ARC, and student ID, as the required documents may vary by plan.

**Official Chunghwa Telecom links:**

- [Chunghwa Telecom official website](https://www.cht.com.tw/en/home/cht)
- [Chunghwa Telecom service locations](https://www.cht.com.tw/en/home/cht/about-cht/business-group/branch)

### Why You Should Get a SIM Card Early

Without mobile data, even a simple trip into the city to collect my ARC was difficult. Mobile data is essential for using Google Maps, checking bus schedules, contacting people, and navigating around Taiwan.

### Alternative: Student Prepaid Plans

If you miss Registration Day or prefer not to sign a monthly contract, consider Chunghwa Telecom's student prepaid plans. These may be useful if you mainly need internet access for your laptop through your phone's hotspot.

| Plan | Price | Average Monthly Cost |

| 30 days unlimited | NT$699 | NT$699 |
| 45 days unlimited | NT$1,050 | NT$700 |
| 60 days unlimited | NT$1,350 | NT$675 |
| 90 days unlimited | NT$1,800 | NT$600 |

*Prices are based on Chunghwa Telecom's student prepaid plan listings. Confirm current prices and availability before purchasing. These plans are described as offering unlimited 4G browsing, which does not necessarily mean unlimited full-speed 5G access.*

**Important hotspot warning:** Unlimited browsing does not automatically mean unlimited hotspot sharing. Before purchasing a plan, ask whether it allows you to share your phone's internet with your laptop and whether hotspot usage has a separate data limit.

You can show the staff this Chinese sentence:

「我想買學生預付卡，主要是要用手機分享網路給筆電。請問這個方案的熱點分享有沒有流量限制？」

*Translation: I want to buy a student prepaid SIM card mainly to share my phone's internet with my laptop. Does this plan have a hotspot data limit?*

### Which Option Should You Choose?

- **Best option:** Consider the NT$600-per-month contract on Registration Day if you qualify and the terms suit your needs.
- **If you miss Registration Day:** Visit a Chunghwa Telecom service center after receiving your ARC and student ID to compare available contract plans.
- **If you prefer flexibility:** Consider the NT$1,800 90-day prepaid student plan, provided the staff confirms that its hotspot allowance is sufficient for your laptop.



## 3. Dorm Life

Prepare basic cleaning supplies, such as a broom, mop, and trash bags. Agree on a cleaning schedule with your roommates so everyone shares the responsibility of keeping the room tidy.

Keep your desk, bed, and personal belongings organized, and remember to do your laundry regularly. Be considerate of your roommates by keeping noise down, especially at night, and respecting each other's personal space.

Your dorm is where you sleep, study, and relax, so keeping it clean and comfortable will make your university life much better.

## 4. Food and Groceries

### If You Want to Cook

Buying groceries at a supermarket outside the campus is generally cheaper than relying on ready-made meals from FamilyMart.

**Recommended supermarket:** Shoufeng Township Farmers' Association Supermarket, Zhixue Store (壽豐鄉農會超市 志學門市).

Search for the Chinese name on Google Maps to find its location.

### If You Don't Want to Cook

You can buy meal boxes from Fried Chicken Master or other nearby food vendors. Many offer different dishes and meal options.

I recommend choosing a meal box with vegetables, eggs, and your preferred main dish to get a more balanced meal with protein and vitamins. Since you will probably eat here regularly, try not to rely only on fried food.

Check the official calendar and course registration system for any changes or additional instructions before each registration period.
